80s trivia walkthrough

Northstar Studio and its players are fictional. These are real screens from demo session 1423.

Start a game while the team settles in. This is Friday Rewind at our fictional company, Northstar Studio. The host opens Quick Start and picks eighties trivia. Engage creates a room with a join code and a QR code. Leave this screen up so everyone can join.

Maya joins on her phone. She points the camera at the QR code and opens the link. The room code is already filled in. She types her name, taps Join Game, and waits for the host. There is no app to install and no account to create.

Ben joins from his laptop. He types the play link with the room code, then enters his name. Back on the host screen, Maya and Ben are ready. More teammates join in the background. Eight players are now in the room. The host starts the first question.

Each player answers on their own screen. Maya picks John Cusack and submits. Ben goes for Jon Cryer. His answer is in too. The host can see how many people have answered without calling on anyone. Once all eight answers are in, the host shows the results.

John Cusack is right. Maya takes the lead, and her phone shows her points and first place. Press S on the host keyboard to show the full scoreboard. All eight players are listed. Press S again to hide it and bring the question back. The host controls the pace.

The game moves on to the next question. Players answer, and the host reveals the result. Before round five, the host opens Session, then Questions, and chooses Ask next beside a question. That choice goes to the front of the queue. The next round uses the question the host picked.

A few more questions keep the room guessing. Here are the answers from rounds five, eight, and ten. The scoreboard stays out of the way during the quiz. After ten rounds, the host brings it back for the final standings. Maya wins Friday Rewind. Everyone can see where they finished.

The results stay with the session. The host opens Session, then Rounds, and opens the report. It brings the round results together for a closer look. Choose Print, then Save as PDF to keep a copy. That is the full flow: start a room, invite the team, play together, and keep the results.

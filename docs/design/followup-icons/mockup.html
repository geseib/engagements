What this needs (option A)

Backend. The asks call gains an anchor per ask (section heading + point number, checked against the stored read) and the three kinds: Ideas (open text), Choose (today's pick one), Rate (1 to 5, replacing agree / not sure / disagree). The prompt asks for at least one ask and up to three. An ask whose anchor does not match the read appears at the end of the read instead of being lost.

Host screens. Icons beside the anchored points on the laptop and the Remote; the preview dialog (the shared dialog look); a new step "Question for the room" with Show results / Cancel; then Next round / Ask another.

Wall and phones. The ask, the answered count, then results as counts (bars for Choose, the average and spread for Rate, host-picked ideas for Ideas). Phones use the existing survey inputs.

Report. Each ask and its results in the round report.

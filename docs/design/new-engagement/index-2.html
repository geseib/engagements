2-04

Call & Answer: the full walkthrough, step by step

The six states of the side pane, at its real width (420px). Each step pairs what the host does, what phones show and what the room screen shows; the last is what you get. Back / Next and the dots move through them; ▶ Play advances every 8 seconds, pauses while the pointer or focus is inside the pane, stops at the last step, and is not offered under reduced motion. Facts checked against help: phones rank their top three; names are hidden while voting and go up with the results; comments are asked for after Workie’s read and carry the writer’s name.

2-05

The other six formats: their walkthrough steps

The same walkthrough, one picture per step, for Trivia, Poll, Wavelength, Survey, Build Room and Event. In the dialog each step is drawn at full pane size like Call & Answer’s; here they are thumbnails. Checked against the help: Poll has no scoreboard and no names; Wavelength has no winners and shows nothing until everyone is in; a Survey opens as it is created; an event holds up to 16 items.

Trivia

Ask a question with one right answer. Everyone picks A, B, C or D; you reveal the answer and a scoreboard keeps count.

  1. ABCD
    1You ask

    A question and its options go up. Phones show A, B, C, D.

  2. 14/18
    2Everyone picks

    Each person taps an answer and locks it in. The screen counts who has answered.

  3. ABC ✓D
    3You reveal

    The right answer is marked and the others dim, with how many chose each. Phones show right or wrong.

  4. 1 Priya 402 Sam 353 Ade 30
    4Scores

    Press S for the scoreboard. Phones show their points and place.

  5. What you get

    Scores, and which questions the room knew or missed.

Poll

Ask a quick question: pick one, rate it, yes or no, or a few words. The results fill in live on the big screen.

  1. ABCD
    1You ask

    A choice, a rating, yes or no, or an open question. Its options go up straight away.

  2. 2Results fill in live

    People answer on their phones and the bars fill in as they do. No names, no right answer.

  3. 3Show results

    The board freezes for everyone to read. Then the next question.

  4. What you get

    Each result, as the room answered it.

Wavelength

Give the room a subject. Everyone writes the words it brings to mind; a word counts when everyone wrote it.

  1. Onboarding
    1You give a subject

    Phones show the subject and ten word boxes.

  2. 14/18
    2Everyone writes words

    Nothing shows until everyone is in. Go for the obvious words the rest will think of too.

  3. docs ×6buddy 4laptop 3wiki 2
    3The shared words

    Spellings and plurals count together. Words on every list stand out; the rest show how many said them.

  4. docsbuddyaccess
    4End of the session

    The screen shows the words the whole room shared. Nobody scores.

  5. What you get

    Where the team already thinks alike, and where it doesn’t.

Survey

Send a short form people fill in on their phones, in their own time. You see the answers added up.

  1. Join code
    4821
    1Open it

    Pressing Open the survey creates it and opens it. The join code and QR go up.

  2. 23 of 36 finished
    2People answer in their own time

    Every question, on their own phone, at their own pace. The screen shows how far the room has got.

  3. 2 minutes left
    3Warn, then close

    A two-minute warning goes to every phone; then you close it.

  4. 4Read it back

    See the results yourself, walk through them with the room, then Workie’s summary.

  5. What you get

    The answers added up, with names only if you chose that.

Build Room

Build something with your Claude Code while the room steers: people suggest ideas, pick between options and rate what Claude makes.

  1. Food bank sign-up
    ✓ Claude Code connected
    1Set it up

    On the Build Room page: the title, the goal (or let the room decide), connect your Claude Code.

  2. AB
    Which sign-up page?
    2Claude asks the room

    Ideas, a choice between options or mockups, or a rating. You check each question first.

  3. AB
    3The room answers

    On phones, tablets or laptops, with the four-digit code.

  4. Direction for Claude:
    Build page A, with B’s big button.
    4You decide

    You turn the room’s answer into a direction and Claude builds it. Then the next question.

  5. What you get

    Something built, every decision the room made, and a report.

Event

Run a whole day behind one join code: an agenda of quizzes, polls, talks and breaks, each with its own report.

  1. 09:00 Welcome09:20 Trivia09:45 Break10:00 Call & Answer
    1Plan the day

    Name, date, start time and place, then the agenda: up to 16 items.

  2. Join code
    4821
    2One code all day

    People join once. Their phones follow the agenda.

  3. 09:00 Welcome09:20 Trivia09:45 Break10:00 Call & Answer
    3You start each item

    Phones go straight into it: a quiz, a poll, a Call & Answer round, a talk or a break.

  4. Platform Offsite
    4Each item reports

    Every session on the agenda keeps its own report, gathered under the event.

  5. What you get

    Each item’s report, gathered under the event.

2-06

Format guide at 1440×900

Opened from Quick view, and still the wide dialog: the guide always takes min(1760px, the window − 48px), whatever mode opened it, and returns to that mode’s width on Back. The 7-column table is replaced by two rows: the chosen format’s walkthrough on top (picture, the step, a rail of all its steps) and seven short cards beneath, one per format, that act as tabs. Every name fits on one line, the group labels sit above their cards, and there is one “Use …” button, in the walkthrough panel beside the format’s name, so it can never be clipped by a column.

## What the screenshot shows (Format guide, opened from the compact dialog, ~1850px-wide window)

- The dialog stays at the compact width while the guide shows, so the seven-column table does not
  fit: the Build Room column is cut off at the right edge ("Build Ro", "Use Build R", "Suggests
  idea…"), Event is not visible at all, and the "Continue on their own screen" group label is cut
  ("CONTINUE (").
- The chosen column's button is clipped: "Use Call & Answ".
- "Call & Answer" wraps onto two lines in its column header.
- Cells are tall, narrow text columns; the window has a lot of unused width either side.
- "Always open with the full guide" at the bottom is the only way back to the full view, and it is
  inside the guide.
- The format descriptions are abstract ("Answers an open question on their phones, then votes for
  the answers that matter") rather than saying plainly what happens in the room.

## What the owner is asking for

1. Open in the guided view with the preview by default, with an obvious, always-visible way to
   switch between the guided and the quick view (remembered).
2. Plain descriptions of what happens, modelled on: "Ask everyone in the room something. Everyone
   reads the responses and votes for their favourites; comments are recorded."
3. Better use of width: the Format guide must show all seven formats without clipping on a laptop
   and use a wide window; the quick view should not feel cramped.
4. A preview that explains what actually happens: multi-step (a short storyboard per format —
   what the host does, what phones show, what the room screen shows, what you get at the end),
   keeping the current visual style.

## Draft copy (from the lead, to use or improve)

See `copy-draft.diff` beside this file; the key lines:

- Call & Answer: "Ask the room a question. Everyone answers on their phone, reads all the answers
  and votes for their favourites." Room: "Answers your question on their phones, reads everyone's
  answers (no names while voting), votes for their favourites, and can comment on what Workie
  says." You get: "Every answer ranked by the room's votes, Workie's read of what the room said, and
  the comments, all in the report."
- Trivia: "Ask a question with one right answer. Everyone picks A, B, C or D; you reveal and
  explain the answer, and a scoreboard keeps count."
- Poll: "Ask a quick question: pick one, rate it, yes or no, or a few words. The results fill in
  live on the big screen." (no vote, no right answer, no names)
- Wavelength: "Give the room a subject. Everyone writes the words it brings to mind; a word scores
  when everyone wrote it." (best with ten or fewer)
- Survey: "Send a short form people fill in on their phones in their own time. You see the answers
  added up."
- Build Room: "Build something with your Claude Code while the room steers: people suggest ideas,
  pick between options and rate what Claude makes."
- Event: "Run a whole day behind one join code: an agenda of quizzes, polls, talks and breaks, each
  with its own report."

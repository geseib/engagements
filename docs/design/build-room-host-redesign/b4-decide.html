1The results stay the room's

Bars, counts and reasons, at the Room ladder, as today. Nothing is drawn over them.

2One sentence, prefilled, editable

The same defaultDirection() prefill as today. It is the decision, so it gets the size and the position of the primary action.

3Everything else folds into Adjust

Chosen, Fold in, What the room said and the Send to Claude switch are today's DecidePanel controls, all kept. The summary line says their current values, so a host who never opens Adjust still knows what will be sent.

4Why a sheet and not the drawer

The container rule: stay inline when a modal would cover the thing being judged. The drawer would cover the right half of the results; the sheet takes only the dock's strip and grows upward.

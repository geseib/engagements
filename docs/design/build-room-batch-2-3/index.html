One main button, always in the same place, with the same words on every screen

Batches 2 and 3 of the consistency review (F1, F2, F3, F8, F11, F12, F16, F27), with the owner's rulings of 2026-10-08. F3: sending the room's clear winner is one press, with "Change before sending" beside it, on the Host screen and the Stage alike. 1Save for later, from the queue

The idea moves from Waiting for you to Later. Its author's device says "The host saved your idea for later." (phone, laptop or tablet alike).

2Each item says what it is

Idea from the room (blue, with who sent it) or Direction for Claude (amber, with where it came from: an ask, or typed). Oldest first. One list, so nothing turns up somewhere the host did not expect.

3Three moves, the same on every item

Send to Claude now (as Do now; the paper plane), Ask the room (opens the Ask the room window with the words filled in), Remove (quiet, left; the row's left slot offers Undo until the next action). All secondary: the one orange button stays with What's next.

4Several at once

Tick two to six and Put to a vote: a Choose ask with each as an option. What's next leads with it when two or more room ideas are waiting in Later.

On the Stage

Nothing. Later is the host's working list. Directions in it may be half-formed, and ideas in it have not been chosen.

In History

An item shows up only when it leaves Later: "Claude was told … (saved for later at 2:47)" when sent, or as the new ask when put to the room. Removed items never appear.

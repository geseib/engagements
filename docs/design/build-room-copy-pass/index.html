Build Room copy pass

A proposal, not a change. Owner, 2026-10-10: follow Orwell's rules, say less, above all on the Stage and in the Build Room; tooltips only where a host needs a sentence once. Nothing here touches code.

The rules this pass kept

  • Labels the vocabulary contract fixes (words.js W and RETIRED) keep their words: Send B to Claude, Change before sending, Save for later, Later, Vote again, Spin the wheel, Main menu, Show results, Open voting, Show on Stage, Talk it over and the rest. Only notes, hints and empty states change.
  • An explanation under a button or a setting is cut to nothing or to six words at most.
  • A sentence the host needs only once moves into a tooltip (marked tooltip, the tooltip text under the proposal).
  • The Stage carries the fewest words: a count, a state, the one move.
  • Laptops, tablets and phones are named only where a device must be: the builder's laptop, the Mac asking about node. Nowhere else.
  • Words are counted by the shown text; {…} stands for a name, a number or a letter and counts as one word. Rows that only say a label again are left out.

Build Room: the console's layout, and considering any idea at any time

For the owner, 19 Oct. Frame 1 answers question (d), the side columns, and shows what (b) "the main button slot" and (c) "the strip with no ask open" mean. Frames 2–6 are the new request: from any list, consider an item, then queue it to the room or send it to Claude. Every item that reached Claude carries a mark, and the mark opens History at the entry, where it shows what was sent, how the host changed it, and why. The laptop uses the console's real type sizes. The session is invented.

1 · The laptop on two screens: four slots on the left, folds on the right (d)

This matches Call & Answer's console as it shipped on 18 Oct. "Your part now" is a card at the top of the centre, and the main button sits in that card. The left column keeps four slots, as Call & Answer's does. Decided, Timeline, Asks, Screenshots and Room brief fold into the right column instead of becoming more slots.

2 · Ideas arriving in an open Ideas ask: Consider on any one of them

The ask runs as normal (collect, vote, settle). The host doesn't have to wait for it: Consider on any idea, as it arrives, opens the panel in frame 3. The same Consider is on answers arriving in a Rate or Choose ask, on every option after a vote, and on cards in Waiting for you.

3 · Consider: queue it for the room, or send it to Claude, with the why

Two doors. Queue for the room keeps the idea's words and lets you pick the round: yes/no, feedback, or add it to a vote. Send to Claude starts from the room's words, and you can change them. When you change them, a "Why?" line asks you to say why, because Claude reads it and History keeps it. The why is optional; the hint suggests one.

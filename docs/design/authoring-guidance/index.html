Authoring guidance: templates, AI prompts and the scenario builder

For the owner, 25 Oct. Two screens change. (a) Every set type gets a prompt to copy into your own chatbot, next to its template, with the three steps written out and a way back to upload. (b) The AI Scenario Builder shows what the AI is asked as read-only text, keeps your own additions in a separate box that starts empty, labels the detail levels by what they produce, and says what is happening and what to do next. The console's type sizes (12 / 13 / 15 / 19 / 24 / 30, rows 36px). The question-sets panel is drawn in dusk, as the console shows it; the builder on white, as its modal is.

a1 · New question set: the template and the chatbot prompt sit together, with the three steps

"Use your own chatbot" was offered for Trivia and Call & Answer only. It is now offered for every type: Trivia, Call & Answer, Poll, Survey, Wavelength, and Art Title (inside the artwork route). The route lists its three steps, so a host knows the template and the prompt go together and that the file comes back here. Show what is copied opens the full prompt before anything goes to the clipboard.

a3 · Every type has a prompt, and each asks for what that type needs

The prompts are built from the same rules the importer checks. Poll and survey take theirs from the survey contract (shared/survey-kinds.js): the columns each kind uses, the allowed values, and the limits, so a poll CSV's 27 columns stop being a mystery. Every prompt ends with the same house rules and "Engage checks every row when I upload it".

TypeThe host fills inWhat the AI is told that it would otherwise get wrong
TriviaTopic, audience, count, categories, difficulty mix, explanation depthFour same-kind options in A–D, E and F left empty; vary the correct letter; only facts it is certain of in the explanation.
Call & AnswerTopic, audience, count, categories, lesson length, toneA concrete lesson; the instruction is a forward-looking question through the team's lens ("What could our team…"), never "Recall a time you…".
Art TitleTheme or period, audience, countLeave Detail_lesson empty (players see it while they invent a title); the real title goes only in AnswerDetails; an image URL only if it is certain, otherwise empty.
WavelengthTopic, audience, count, categoriesA subject of 1–4 words and nothing else: no description (it seeds the very words the round compares); the instruction cell is fixed.
PollTopic, audience, count, categories, which kindsKind is choice, rating, yesno or text; the columns each kind uses and leaving the rest empty; each row in a category the host can switch off.
SurveyWhat you want to find out, audience, count (up to 20), which kindsAll five kinds incl. rank; Category is always Survey; a follow-up needs its question; neutral wording, one thing per question.

b1 · Scenario builder: what the AI is asked, and your additions, apart

Today the box "Base Prompt & Additional Requirements" is pre-filled with the card's prompt, so the prompt reaches the AI twice; on a built-in card the browser also writes context, audience and level into it, and the server adds them again. Now What the AI is asked shows the card's prompt read-only, with where it comes from. Your additions starts empty and is sent on its own. The detail levels say what they produce; all three stay inside the same length limit.

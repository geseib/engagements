On the wall

A survey collecting at its own pace, then the host walking the room through the results one question at a time. A Poll reveals exactly these result screens after each question, because a poll is the same five kinds, host-paced. Room profile, 1920×1080.

Design notes
The audited stage, unchangedRail, phase band, fitter, dock, the four ladders and every floor are stage-base.css + the 22 Sep refresh, inlined verbatim. The survey sheet adds two shapes (histogram, split) and reuses the rest.
Collecting is not ASKNobody is looking up during a self-paced survey, so the wall's job is the way in (QR and code stay) and a sense of pace: finished of joined, and answered per question. No arrivals: a survey's words wait for close.
Most picked, not CorrectTrivia's reveal (bars grow, the row lands, the flag slides in) with the flag in amber and the word changed. A survey has no right answer, so green would lie.
Walking the room through itAfter close, the dock pages through results (Previous / Next result). No wipe between them — it is not a phase change — so the reveal starts almost at once (--rv:.15s).
Quotes without namesThe featured quote is the feedback wall's blockquote.featured. A comment carries its author because the author was told; a survey answer never does.
Ends with WorkieThe last result's primary is ‘What we heard’, the stage's existing field-notes beat, now reading Workie's survey read.

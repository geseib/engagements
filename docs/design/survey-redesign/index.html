Surveys and polls

Five question kinds — rating, multiple choice, yes/no, ranking, open answer — shared by two ways of running them. A Survey is a form people fill in at their own pace; results come together when the host closes it, and a Names setting decides whether it is Anonymous, records Who finished, or is Named. A Poll is the same questions one at a time, each result revealed on the wall. Every page is built from the approved stylesheets (admin console, player, stage, report) with a small survey sheet on top; press N on any page to hide the design notes. See RATIONALE.md and PLAN.md beside this file.

Create and edit

  • New surveySurvey joins the kinds of round; four ways in: your material, a template, blank, a file.
  • Generate from your materialPaste the outline or attach the deck; say what you want to find out; pick the kinds; pick how many.
  • Review the draftThe shared review table with a Kind column; keep, edit, rewrite one, change its kind; saved as a draft set.
  • The editorThe set editor's Questions tab for a survey: kind, question, answer preview; the Add question menu open.
  • Edit a questionOne form whose fields follow the kind, with the real player beside it as the preview.
  • The fields each kind asks forMultiple choice, Yes / No, Ranking and Open answer, side by side.
  • Start a survey sessionNames — Anonymous, Who finished or Named — fixed once it opens; the results link's two-day clock starts at open.

Answer

  • Answering on a phone (board)Rating 1–5, rating 0–10, multiple choice, yes/no with its why, ranking, open answer.
  • Finishing, and results coming back (board)Check and send, the thank-you, the ENDED screen with results, the shared page.
  • Names (board)What each phone promises under the three settings; who's left on the wall, on request.

On the wall

  • On the wall (board)Collecting at its own pace, then a result screen per kind — the same screens a poll reveals.

Results and sharing

  • ResultsWorkie's read on top; a chart per question, each with Workie's one-line note.
  • Open answersThemes as filters, every answer readable, put one on the wall, held-back answers that name a person.
  • Share the resultsA frozen, read-only copy: anyone with the link, two days from when the survey opened; three ways out — wall, phones, link.
  • PeopleNamed: who finished, who stopped partway, and each person's answers. Names stop at the console.
  • The PDF reportThe survey section of the paper report, in GameReport's own type.

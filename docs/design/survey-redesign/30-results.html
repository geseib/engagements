Workie’s read

of all 41 responses

People came away with something to use — and want more room to talk back.

Three in four rated the session useful (4 or 5 of 5), and the live demo is the reason they give most: it was picked as the most valuable part by more than a third, and it is the biggest theme in what people wrote. The length split the room less than the recommend score suggests — most of the eleven who said ‘too long’ want the same forty minutes spent differently, not fewer of them.

What to do next

  1. Give questions twenty minutes, not five — it is the top format ask (61%) and the top written one.
  2. Send the deck the day before. Twelve asked for it outright; it also answers most of the ‘cut the roadmap’ notes.
  3. Keep a live demo in every all-hands. Nothing else scored like it.

Workie's read, reusing the workieVoice = the persona picker, Approach = the summary-prompt picker, both as the session setup and the stage already offer them (584c11f6). New: a survey default prompt (none ships today; default-ai-prompts.json has only call-and-answer and trivia) and one structured call that also writes the per-question notes and the themes. Redo takes a steer, the same way the stage's Redo rewrites the one on screen.
A chart per kind, and only fiveRating = histogram + mean (a scale is ordered); 0–10 = the recommend split; choice = bars with Most picked; yes/no = one split bar and the why; ranking = average place with where-it-landed strips; open answer = themes with a quote. Every bar has its number printed; colour never carries a result alone.

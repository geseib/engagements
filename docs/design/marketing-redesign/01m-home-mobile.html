Two ways to play

One set of questions, two shapes of round.

Trivia

Warm the room up, or check what landed.

A question, four options, one right answer — revealed with its explanation.

  • Ask, then results. Trivia has no vote phase.
  • Running standings after every question.
Call and answer

Pose a prompt, collect every idea, then vote.

Everyone writes at once. Then the room reads the answers and votes on them.

  • Ask, vote, results.
  • Every answer is kept, with its vote count.

4

Your organisation’s private library, encrypted — or the moderated public one.

Design notes — 01m home at 390px
The phone clip goes UNDER the TV clipNot beside it at a quarter of the size. At 390px the front-room screen is the thing being explained and the player’s phone is the follow-up, so they are read in that order.
The nav keeps Sign in, drops CreateA returning host signs in from a phone; nobody fills in a registration form standing in a corridor. Create a host account is still one tap away inside the menu, and it is the hero’s first button.
The join card is the whole widthFour 54px cells plus a Join button fit 390 − 2×16 with room to spare, so the compact entry does not need a second layout.
The hero still splits 55/45The headline stays in the upper band and the glow in the lower one at this width too. The band is 620px here rather than 92vh so the frame can be reviewed on a desktop.

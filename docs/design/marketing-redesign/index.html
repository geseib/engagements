Marketing home and its related pages — mockups

The signed-out / today is a bare four-digit code entry. These six pages replace it with a marketing surface aimed at team leaders and facilitators, and define the slots that sub-projects 2 (screen clips) and 3 (help content) fill. Read RATIONALE.md for why each page is shaped the way it is.

Serve this directory. From file:// the audit cannot reach inside the frames. Press N on any mockup to hide the design-notes rail; it is hidden automatically under 1440px.

The pages

  1. Home — the climb

    Hero over the ridge scene with the compact Have a code? entry, the problem, the two ways to play, your own material, the room reacting, and the paper report at the summit.

  2. Home at 390px

    The same markup and the same classes in a 390px column: single column, phone clip stacked under the TV clip, nav collapsed to brand + Sign in + menu.

  3. /how-it-works

    Six steps with their clip slots, alternating sides, then the four display profiles and the phone remote.

  4. /use-cases

    Four scenarios — offsite, retrospective, decision workshop, trivia warm-up — each a before / with pair, the set type used, and the same two CTAs.

  5. /reports

    A full paper report with six numbered callouts, then export and sharing, then anonymity.

  6. /help

    The role and guide sidebar beside a guide body, at /help/<role>/<guide>.

Six assertions over all six pages at 1440×900, 1280×800 and 390×844: no horizontal scroll, nothing clips silently, the 12px floor, WCAG AA contrast composited up the ancestor chain, target size, and no unsized icon. It is the admin console's audit.html with the page list changed and one exemption added, noted in the file.

What is shared

mk.css carries the token block that Task 4 copies verbatim into MarketingShell.css, plus every .mk-* and .jce-* rule the React will reuse. _ridge.html is the scene markup the six pages share; it is a reference copy, not a page. notes.js is the design-notes toggle and is not part of the product.

No raster image, no webfont and no third-party origin is loaded by any of these files. The ridge scene and every product screen inside a device bezel are inline SVG and plain CSS, which is the constraint the removed hero scene broke when it hot-linked three layer images from a third-party CDN.

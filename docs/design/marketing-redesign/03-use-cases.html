Use cases

Four sessions people actually run.

Each one is the same product with a different set of questions. The difference is the material you bring.

01 · Strategy offsite

Two days, forty opinions, one plan

Set type: call and answer, drafted from the strategy paper everyone was sent and nobody finished.

Before

The loudest table sets the agenda by lunchtime. Three flip charts of sticky notes go home in a phone photo and are never opened.

With Engagements

Every person answers each prompt from their seat, so the quiet half is on the screen too. The room votes on what it has just read. The report is circulated before anyone reaches the car park.

02 · Retrospective

What we stop, start and keep

Set type: call and answer, three prompts, drafted from the last two retro notes.

Before

People wait to hear what the manager thinks before saying what they think. The same two items are raised every sprint and never resolved.

With Engagements

Everyone writes at the same moment, before anyone has heard anyone else. Names can be hidden for the whole session. The vote decides which item the team carries, and the report shows the ones it did not.

03 · Decision workshop

Three options, one room, a record of why

Set type: call and answer, one prompt per option, drafted from the options paper.

Before

The decision is made in the room and the reasoning stays in the room. Six weeks later nobody can say which objections were raised and answered.

With Engagements

Each option collects its own answers and its own vote. The report keeps the objections with the option they were raised against, including the ones that won no votes.

Create a host account See the report
04 · Team trivia warm-up

Ten minutes that wake the room up

Set type: trivia, ten questions, drafted from the onboarding handbook and last quarter’s numbers.

Before

The first twenty minutes of the day are spent waiting for people to stop reading email. An icebreaker with no connection to the work.

With Engagements

Ten questions about your own material, with the explanation on the results screen. People arrive knowing what the day is about, and the standings give the room something to laugh at.

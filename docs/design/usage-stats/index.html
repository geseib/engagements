Notes

Counted, created, started

Counted uses the billing rule: two different questions answered. Created and started sit beside it so a rehearsal is visible without inflating the lead number.

Participants

One per new seat in a session, written once when the seat is made. Reconnects, refreshes and handovers reuse the seat. Two sessions of 10 and 2 people read 2 sessions, 12 participants.

Kept today

Sessions not yet expired, from the daily job. For a closed month it is the month-end figure.

Events

One line, not a type row: events created, run, and people registered. Their item sessions already count in the type rows; adding them again would double them.

One orange

Download CSV is each screen's only orange. Current nav, links, the staff bar and the counting-since note are blue or neutral.

Not shown

Titles of sessions, team sets' names to staff, questions, answers. The stats rows hold ids and counts only.

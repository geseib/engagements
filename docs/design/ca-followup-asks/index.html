After the read, one more question for the room

Owner, 2026-10-11. When Workie has read the round back, it also suggests up to three short questions the host can send to everyone's phone, laptop or tablet: agree or disagree with a line the room wrote, pick one of the room's own options, or answer one open question. The host picks one, edits it if they like, and sends it. Results come back to the Stage and go into the report. The wording in these frames is what the prototype actually returned (PROTOTYPE.md), from round 3 of the agentic SDLC set.

Nothing new appears while the read is being read. On its last page, where the dock today offers Request feedback, it offers Ask the room… instead, with a count when suggestions are ready. The feedback round is not lost: it is one of the choices inside (F2). Next round stays the one amber button. The Host Remote gets the same list in the second build.

Send to the room is the one amber button; Back and the X leave without sending, through one close that asks first when something was edited.

Open questions (full list, with recommendations, in SPEC.md)

  1. The projector sees the drawer. The host's laptop is usually the Stage, so the suggestions are visible while the host chooses. They come from words the room has already seen. Recommended: accept it, and offer the same list on the Host Remote for a host who wants to choose in private.
  2. "Ask the room…" in place of "Request feedback". Recommended: yes, with Comments on the read inside it, so the dock keeps two buttons.
  3. Agree scale. Recommended: three answers (Agree, Not sure, Disagree), not a five-point scale.
  4. Open questions in the first build? Recommended: second build. They scored well, but they put free text on the wall.
  5. Reworded suggestions. Recommended: drop an agree statement that is not the room's words; show a reworded option or question marked Reworded.

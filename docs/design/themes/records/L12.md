# L12: Notice

New component, built to SPEC §6 and PLAN §3. Styling only: `StatusMessage` keeps its behaviour
(tone from the words, `role="status"`, `aria-live`) and is not edited. No screen adopts Notice in
this phase. Committed on `skins/L-g2` (the group branch), not `skins/L12`.

API: `<Notice tone='ok'|'warn'|'danger'|'note' icon='<Phosphor name>' as='div' className …rest>`.
Default `tone='note'`. StatusMessage's tone names are accepted and mapped by `noticeTone()`:
`success→ok`, `error→danger`, `pending→note`; anything else → `note`. `icon` renders
`components/Icon` (fill weight, 1.15em) in an `aria-hidden` span coloured with the tone's hue.
Words go in `.ui-notice-body`. `role`/`aria-live`/`id` pass through. Size is the caller's
(`var(--ui-size, 1em)`).

SPEC ambiguities resolved:
- **`--tint-ok/-warn/-danger/-note` do not exist.** SPEC §6 names them as roles Notice reads;
  roles.css never declared them (0a). Each tone builds its tint from the channel roles instead,
  which resolve per skin exactly like a role. If the roles are added later, the four
  `--ui-notice-tint` lines are the whole change.
- **The alpha is .08, one for every tone.** The tint lowers the words' contrast: on dusk
  `--danger-text` over a .10 red tint on `--surface-2` is 4.49:1 (under AA), at .12 4.39; at
  .08 it is 4.58. The .45 edge carries the hue.
- **Ink per theme**, same mechanism and reason as L8 (record `L8.md`): every tone's text role
  is under AA on paper in Classic, so paper words are `--text` (danger `--danger-deep`), keyed on
  the nearest `[data-theme]` by `--ui-notice-<tone>-ink` blocks declared at `:root,
  [data-theme="dark"]` then `[data-theme="light"]`.
- **Note is `--info`, not the accent**: a neutral/pending notice is not a selection, so Present
  keeps it blue and Classic keeps it blue.
- **Note ink on dusk is `--text`**, not `--info` (SPEC's "matching *-text" has no `--info-text`;
  `--text` is 8.92:1 at worst).

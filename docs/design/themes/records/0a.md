### The migration shim in roles.css

`components/TalkingPointsReport.css:18` reads `var(--info, var(--text))`. `--info` did not exist,
so the Build Room report's source links rendered in `--text`; declaring the role would have
turned them `#7CA7E6` on paper (2.6:1, and a Classic change). `roles.css` ends with
`.report-paper .tpr { --info: var(--text); }`, commented as a shim; delete it when that sheet's
unit rewrites the declaration (to `var(--text)`, or to a paper link colour). Pinned in
`skinPalette.test.js`.

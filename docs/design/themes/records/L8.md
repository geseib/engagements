No `keep:`.

SPEC ambiguity resolved — **the paper ink, and how a component picks it.** PLAN asks for every
tone AA on `--bg` and `--surface` in every skin x theme. On paper no tone colour clears AA in
Classic (`--phase-ask`/`--warn` 1.96:1 on white; Classic's paper `--success-text` and
`--danger-text` are the dusk values, §3 amendments), so on paper the word is `--text`
(`--danger-deep` for danger, SPEC's paper destructive ink) and the tone is carried by the
outline and the dot. Dusk keeps the stage idiom (the word in the phase colour), so a later
adoption of `.chip.ask/.vote/.results` stays Classic-identical on dusk.

A descendant selector cannot choose by the NEAREST themed ancestor (`<html>` always carries
`data-theme="light"`, so `[data-theme="light"] .ui-chip` matches every chip). The inks are
therefore declared exactly where roles.css declares the roles — `:root, [data-theme="dark"]`,
then `[data-theme="light"]` — as `--ui-chip-<tone>-ink`, resolved on the themed element and
inherited. These are the only selectors in the sheet not rooted at `.ui-chip`; the palette test
holds the order, the name set (only `--ui-chip-*-ink`, the same six in both), and that nothing
else is declared there. If the coordinator would rather have theme-keyed status inks as roles
(`--ok-ink`, …) in roles.css, these two blocks are the whole change.

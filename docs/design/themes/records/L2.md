SPEC ambiguities resolved here:
- **"The surface's own ladder var".** The surfaces' ladders have different names (`--t-hero` on the stage, `--L-hero * --fit` on the wall, `--adm-t-title` in the console). Heading reads one hook per size, `--ui-t-<size>`, which the surface maps once from its own ladder (examples in `Heading.css`'s header). These hooks inherit on purpose: a ladder applies to a whole surface. Unset = `1em`, never a guessed px.
- **Default size.** SPEC gives no default; `title` for an `h1`, `head` for `h2`-`h4` (the laptop ladder's screen title and panel heading).
- **Weight.** Always `--display-weight` (800 in both skins). `.present-h2` uses 700; Phase 2 decides whether it adopts Heading or keeps its class override.
- **Line height** is a hook, `--ui-heading-leading` (default 1.1), because the wall (1.04) and the stage `.q` (1.05) differ to the hundredth.

Choices that need a word:

- **`--rule-strong` is written as a channel, not as the role.** All 30 `rgba(155,168,190,.34)`
  declarations become `rgb(var(--slate-rgb) / .34)`, not `var(--rule-strong)`. The role is paper
  navy `.28` under `data-theme="light"`. Several of these scopes can sit under a light ancestor:
  `<html data-theme="light">`, portaled dialogs, PlanLimitNotice, CountField, the theme-agnostic
  SetReviewBanner, and QuestionImageField, which sets no theme on purpose. With the channel,
  Classic is identical in both themes (classicEquivalence `--theme light`: 0). The cost is that
  Present's dusk `.40` does not reach these scopes yet. A rule wave that proves a scope is dusk
  can change it to the role.
- **Amber tints.**
  - `warn` for warnings and statuses: `*-tint-warn`, the acting-as banner, `--evb-rule-warn` and
    `--evb-tint-cap`, the plan-limit notice, and the prompt manager's "silent" tier.
  - `accent` for the share callout and the preview accent.
  - `raw:var(--row-selected)` for every `*-row-sel` (`rgba(246,169,76,.10)`, exactly Classic's
    `--row-selected`).
- **`--pc-silent`.** It was `var(--accent)` in the first commit and is now `var(--warn)`. It is a
  severity tier, and under Present's blue it measured 4.44:1 on its own tint.
- **`--primary` re-declared on a scope** (`.qs-editor`, `.smed`) becomes `var(--action)`. That is
  exactly the global alias (`--primary: var(--action)`), so the scope matches the rest of the site
  in both skins until its rules are migrated.
- **`#7CA7E6` as `--qs-chip-waiting-ink`, and blue tints,** become `info`. Classic is unchanged.
- **Self-reference trap.** The codemod auto-proposed `--danger: var(--danger)`,
  `--danger-text: var(--danger-text)` and `--danger-deep: var(--danger-deep)` on `.qs-editor` and
  `.smed`. Each of those is a cycle, which makes the value guaranteed-invalid, and
  classicEquivalence caught it. The fix: `--danger` becomes `rgb(var(--danger-rgb))`, and the
  other two are kept. **Tool note for the coordinator:** `codemod.js` should refuse
  `--x: var(--x)`.

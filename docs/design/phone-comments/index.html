Design · 1 Nov 2026 · participant phone

Comments on the phone: make it obvious what each one is about

The owner: “when the participants are asked to comment it is not super apparent the comment buttons go with AI read vs responses. the headers for the sections are the smallest text and both comment ‘buttons’ appear to go with the responses … when they pick response, are they commenting on all responses or each separately. how do we know which response they are commenting on.”

The Today frames use the shipped CSS copied word for word, so the shipped faults show up by themselves rather than being drawn in. The Proposed frames use the player phone’s type ladder (24 / 19 / 16 / 13 px) and its colour tokens. The decisions, the backend changes they need and the reviews are in README.md and reviews.md, next to this file. The sample round is invented.

1 · What ships today

This is the player’s own panel, opened from Feedback on the results screen (PlayerPage.jsx:4661-4671 → 3604-3652 → FeedbackRoundPanel → RoundReport). The room opens the same panel when the host starts a feedback round (PlayerPage.jsx:4414).

2 · Proposed: three sections, each with its own action inside it

There are three real headings at 19px, the second rung of the ladder: Workie’s read, The responses and, last, Anything else about this round? Each action sits inside its own section and says in words what it is about. Each response has its own Reply to this response, and there is no global button over the list. Comments show under the thing they are about. The question at the top is context and takes no comments, as before.

3 · Writing a comment: the sheet says what it is about

The sheet docks to the bottom of the phone shell, as the answer dock does (PlayerSurface.css’s .plr-dock, a flex row and not position: fixed). It is not a Modal, so the page above can still be scrolled and read. Its heading names the target, a quote shows it, and one line gives the name rule. Focus goes to the box. Its accessible name is the heading and its description is the name rule. The X and Cancel do the same thing.

4 · After sending: say what it is attached to, and show it there

5 · Small phone, 360 wide

This is the narrowest common Android width. The headings wrap instead of truncating, the action rows wrap instead of clipping (margin-left: auto plus flex-wrap, never justify-content: flex-end), and every target stays 44px.

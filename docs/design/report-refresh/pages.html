About this session

What the room was asked to do

Half-day offsite for the platform engineering group. We are deliberately setting today’s constraints aside to find out what the team would build if staffing and budget were not the question.

From the session’s details

Three rounds. Answer on your phone, read everyone’s answers, then vote for your top three. Be specific — a named thing beats a category.

01

Round 1 of 3 / Big Bets

If we had unlimited people and resources, what should we build?

Forget the roadmap and the headcount plan for a minute. If money and people were not the constraint, what is the one thing this team should build — and why would it matter?

The taskName the thing in one line, then vote for the three you would fund.
Workie’s readAI analysis of the responses — not the host’s words

The room split between delight and drudgery. The winning answer — a one-week tech-debt remover sold as a service — was the only one that named a customer who would pay, and it was the first choice of five of eight people.

What stood out

  • The tech-debt remover read as a joke and was voted as a strategy: three people argued it is already what the platform team does, unbilled.
  • The grass-seeding robot was the most concrete idea, and the only one with a physical-world customer.
  • The Rube Goldberg machine and the AI fax machine were voted as morale, not as bets.

Questions to take back

  1. If the tech-debt remover is what we already do, why is it invisible on the roadmap?
  2. What would a fixed one-week engagement have to exclude to be sellable?

Next steps

  1. Write a one-page offer for a fixed one-week debt sprint; test it with two internal teams.
  2. Track debt work as its own line so its value shows next quarter.

1Back to where you were

The button names the place: "Back to Ask 4" (History), "Back to the build", "Back to the Host screen", "Back to the vote" (Stage). Esc does the same. The screen behind is exactly as it was.

2Flip, don't reopen

Tabs for every choice, arrows, and ← →. The picked one says so. Works on a laptop, tablet or phone (swipe on touch).

R3 · Stage at results: decide without leaving

The dock carries the two moves the host needs on the wall: Edit and To Claude. To Claude sends the room's choice as it stands. The Space hint reads as an instruction, not a key cap.

2To Claude, and a plain hint

"To Claude: B" sends the room's choice with its default direction, as Do now. The hint is words, "Press Space to send", in the muted label style — no box around it. A tie or no votes: the primary is Spin the wheel, and To Claude waits for a pick.

R4 · Stage: Edit

The Send to Claude panel from the Host screen, as a window over the Stage. Pick between the options, edit the words, choose the kind, then Send, Save for later, Re-ask, or Discard.

1Pick between them, and look first

Click an option to switch the pick (the direction rewrites; a different pick than the room's says so, as today). "view mockup" opens the viewer (R2); Back returns here.

2Every way out, on the wall

Send to Claude (the kind chosen above) · Save for later (For Claude, later: held until you send it) · Re-ask (R5) · Discard (asks first). Close or Esc keeps the results as they are.

R5 · Edit: re-ask, edited first

Re-ask turns the window into the question, filled in from this one: edit the words and the options, then Ask again. The old ask closes as "asked again as Ask 5", as a revote does today.

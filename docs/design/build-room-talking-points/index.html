From docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-build-room-talking-points-design.md. Owner, 2026-10-08: a researcher button, an ideas button, a list the host draws on, a vote where people pick more than one, then highlight the ones to move forward and send them to Claude or work through them one by one. The rules from batches 2 and 3 hold: one orange button on each screen, the main button last on the right of a pinned row, the words of words.js, and Save for later as the only way into Later. Points reach the Stage and people's laptops, tablets and phones only when the host shows them or puts them to a vote.

2Research… and Ideas… at the top

4What's next can point here

When three or more new points are waiting and nothing is open, What's next offers "Talk over a point while Claude builds" as a secondary move. It never leads: the room is not shown anything the host has not chosen.

Who holds the orange

While points are ticked, the row's main button (Send to Claude with one ticked, Put to a vote with two or more) is the one orange button and Space presses it; What's next's lead turns to an outline button until the ticks are cleared. While an ask is open, its step keeps the orange and the row's main button is outline. A test counts orange buttons in each of these states.

Disabled says why

A disabled move stays in the row, so the host can see what more ticks would allow. Ticks across batches

Ticks may span batches and builders. Ticking a group's heading ticks all its points.

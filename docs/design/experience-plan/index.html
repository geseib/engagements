They show the one live-session frame, what stays put across formats, how one-screen mode keeps private things off the room's screen, the participant's side of moving a name to a new device, the visual primitives, the same frame in every format, the one person row, and every format again in one-screen mode. Violet pins and dashed violet outlines are annotations, not product UI.

1 · The one host frame: two screens, Call & Answer, answering, with a name request

1366×768, the host's private laptop. The room's window is on the projector. Sam has joined from a new device and asked for their name.

How to read this sheet

Never moves

Everything without a dashed outline is identical in all seven: the top bar (Main menu, name, Join, the room's window with its green dot, saying "off" only when it is off, Needs you, ?, Session menu), Your part now's place and its one orange button, the Needs-you line at one height, People on the left, three pulse tiles, the content area's box.

The format fills

Dashed violet: the steps strip, the words and label in Your part now, the pulse's three facts, the content area, and People's one added fact (the column outline). Poll and Wavelength have no outline there: nobody scores, so the slot stays empty.

Calm when empty
✓Nothing needs you now.

One muted line, no buttons, same height as the amber state, so nothing below it jumps when a request arrives. Five of the seven are calm here. Build Room and the event item show it amber: Claude's new question, and a name request that carried across the agenda.

Added or taken away, never moved
  • Survey has no live steps, so its strip shows the schedule, and its Session menu loses Phone remote and Rounds and gains Close survey.

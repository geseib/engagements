The Session panel: the same sidebar as the other engagements, with the people in the room first

Owner, 2026-10-09: the Build Room needs the sidebar the other engagements have, with everyone in the room, unlocking a name so a person can move to another device, and removing people, but organised better than their Settings tab. And from the same day: a person asked to move devices, and the host screen never told the host. This page draws the panel built from the one the other engagements use (components/stage/SessionSetupPanel.jsx), with the same words: Unlock name, Let them take it, Remove, Bring back. The rules from batches 2 and 3 hold: one orange button on each screen, the main button last on the right, Main menu first on the left. The panel itself never carries orange.

Checking orange buttons…

Measuring…

1What a row says

Name; Builder for a crew member, with the lane they are on; Here or Away 6 min (a hollow dot, so it does not rely on colour); when they joined; ideas sent and votes cast, as counts only. Ideas come from the ideas the room object already holds; votes per person need checking before they are promised (if they are not cheap, the row says ideas only).

2Unlock name, Let them take it

The other engagements' one button whose meaning changes. With nobody asking it reads Unlock name: the next device to join with that name may take it, once (for "I'm moving to my laptop", said out loud). When somebody asked from their device it reads Let them take it, and only that device can use it. While a name is unlocked the row says so and offers Lock again (new: the other engagements cannot undo an unlock).

3Remove, Bring back

Remove takes a person out of the live counts at once, with no confirm, because Bring back undoes it. Their ideas and votes stay in the report. Removed people sit in their own list below, never mixed in, as in the other engagements. A removed builder: see the questions at the end.

4Nothing orange

The panel is a list of decisions about people, none of which should be pressed by reflex. The orange stays with the screen's own lead move, dimmed behind the panel. Let them take it is the strongest button here: filled, not outlined.

S6 · Someone asks to move to another device

1What is known, and what each answer does

The name asked for; that its holder is here now (or away, which makes a move likelier) and when they joined. One sentence on each answer. No client id and no device detail reach the host: the server keeps those.

2Three answers

Let them take it grants one handover to the device that asked (the other engagements' bindToRequester) and the strip goes. Not now puts the strip away; the ask stays flagged in Players and the count stays on Session until answered there. See in Players opens the panel at the row. Space does not answer it: Space stays with What's next, so a host advancing the room cannot hand a name over by accident.

3The count on Session

On every screen with a header, while any ask is open. Two asks at once: the strip reads "2 people are asking to take a name" and offers See in Players only.

2Lock again and "moved device"

Call and Answer: one round, moment by moment

Version 3 of the proposed flow for a Call and Answer round in Engage. It answers the external review of session 7924 (the "Relay expansion" run, six rounds, four participants) and redraws the reviewers' concept with the team's decisions applied. Every screen below is a wireframe: it shows what each surface shows, where things sit, and the one next step at each moment. Colours and fonts are not the point. Version 3 adds the owner's decisions of 14 October: the round's workflow is always on the host's screens, and setup helps the host choose how the room will watch.

Prepared 14 October 2026 for the product owner, then the external reviewers. Sample content is from the review session; names are the reviewers' fictional participants.

What changed since the reviewers' version

  • One "Ask the room" composer, two ways in. Blank from the dock, or filled in by Workie from a small icon beside a point of the read. Same screen either way; the words are editable at once, then "Send to the room".
  • Your format cards are kept as the composer's kind switch (Ideas, Choose, Rate, Comments). "Comments" replaces "Request feedback", so there is one door for asking the room anything.
  • The end of the read is a real stop. On the last page the main button becomes "Ask the room". Mid-read, "Start round 3" (the next round, by number) needs a second press while questions are still unsent. Paging stays in the fixed button bar, because clickers and the Space key depend on it.
  • Your route strip is kept, redrawn. One slim strip on the host's screens: Setup, the round's loop (Answer, Vote, What we heard, Ask), Finish. The current step is lit and a "Your part now" line names the main button. Labels stay short ("Start voting", "Show results").
  • The workflow can be looked at, not driven. Pressing a step shows it privately; the room, the wall and the phones stay where they are, and "Back to the room" returns.
  • Nothing that arrives late is lost. Late answers, ideas and comments are kept and marked "Not reviewed" until the host reviews them or reviews them with the room.
  • Anyone can go back to an earlier round, see all of it and comment, on their phone, laptop or tablet, or from the host's Rounds menu. Each comment names its target. The wall shows nothing unless the host puts it up.
  • The report is a full record of each question for the room: Workie's words against what was sent, where in the read it came from, timings, results and how many answered.
  • Kept from your version: the real task on the wall, the room-screen choice before round 1 (now with "Help me pick"), settings that say when they apply, "Start round 3", "Preparing questions" then "3 asks ready", "Checking your answer…" after a reload, and a privacy line for each kind.

Changes since version 2

  1. The workflow is always on the host's screens. A slim strip under the title bar on the laptop, a row of dots on the Remote, a slimmer strip on one screen. Never on the wall or the room's devices. (Version 2 had dropped it.)
  2. "Your part now". The strip names what is expected of the host, in the same words as the main button. It adds no buttons.
  3. Look without moving the room. Pressing a step opens a private view of it; an orange bar says where the room really is, and the main button becomes "Back to the room".
  4. Setup helps you pick the room screen. The two drawings of one and two screens, a plain line for each, and "Help me pick": three questions, one recommendation.
  5. Players, Questions, Rounds, Settings stay in reach on every host screen. Settings say when a change applies: now, from the next round, or not while the room is live.
  6. "Next round" names the round ("Start round 3"), and Finish can be previewed privately before the session ends.
  7. Earlier rounds show the whole round on phones, tablets and laptops: the question, your response, all responses by votes, Workie's read, questions for the room and all feedback. The host's Rounds menu shows the same.
  8. Comments say what they are about ("[Theo commented on Maya's response]"), carry one of three labels (Reviewed with the room, Reviewed by the host, Not reviewed), and can be sent anonymously. Two report settings decide what the report includes.

How to read this page

Each moment shows the surfaces side by side. A coloured square in each caption says which surface it is. The orange bar above each row is the one next step for the host at that moment.

Host laptop

The host's main controls. Under the title bar, the workflow strip: where the round is and what is expected next. At the bottom, a fixed button bar: a status line, a second (quiet) button and one main button.

Host phone (Remote)

An optional remote control on the host's own phone. Same buttons, same rules as the laptop, and the same workflow as a row of dots. It also carries the host-only notes.

The wall (Presentation window)

What the room sees on the projector or TV. Never shows host controls, names in anonymous moments, or anything not yet sent.

Participant

Each person in the room, on their own phone, laptop or tablet. Drawn phone-sized; the same page fits a laptop or tablet.

Report

The session report the host opens, saves and shares after the session.

Two screens (the main reference here)

The laptop is the host's private dashboard. The room watches a separate Presentation window on a second screen, opened with "Open Present". Host-only things (Workie's reasons, the icons beside points, late items) stay on the laptop.

One screen (marked One screen)

The laptop itself is projected, so the room sees whatever the laptop shows. The flow is the same, with less on the laptop: no icons beside points, Workie's reasons folded away, late items shown as a count until opened. The Remote, if open, carries the host-only detail.

These two drawings now also appear in setup, where the host chooses the room screen (moment 0 below).

Can a step be pressed?

Yes, but only to look. Pressing a step opens a private view of it: an earlier step shows what happened, Setup shows the session's settings, Finish shows a preview of the report, a later step explains what will happen. Nothing is sent, opened or closed. Every act that changes the room stays in the one button bar. This follows the owner's updated spec ("a viewed step is not a command to rewind participants").

While looking

An orange bar under the strip says "Viewing Setup · the room is still on round 2, What we heard, page 4". The step being looked at has an orange dashed outline; the live step keeps its live dot. The main button becomes "Back to the room", which returns to the live view on the same page. The wall and every phone, laptop and tablet stay on the live step.

How it fits the button bar

Strip: where the round is, and what is expected of the host (fixed words per step). Status line: the live counts and what the room will see next ("3 of 4 voted · Next on the screen: the results"). Main button: the one act. "Your part now" always ends with the main button's label, so the eye goes from the strip to the button.

07

Open questions for the owner

Each has a recommended answer, and the drawings above assume it. None is decided yet.

Proposed, pending the owner

1. Ideas in the report: all of them, or only the picked ones?

List every idea, anonymous, in two groups: "Shown to the room" and "Not shown". Freeze them on the question when it closes so they outlast the short-lived answer rows. Keep the wall picked-only.

This differs from the reviewers' "selected only" reading. Today's product keeps only picked ideas.

Proposed, pending the owner

2. On one screen, should questions for the room appear on the projected laptop?

Yes. Open the composer on the laptop and fold away Workie's "For you" reason. Also add the lobby's "Room screen" choice, so hosts find two screens without a hidden setting.

Decided by the owner, 14 October

3. Review labels

Three labels, for comments and late items: "Reviewed with the room" (shown on the wall), "Reviewed by the host", "Not reviewed". Not-reviewed items are in the report by default; the host can leave them out.

Proposed, pending the owner

4. How long after the session ends can people add comments or late answers?

Comments until 24 hours after the end, with a host button "Close comments" to end it sooner. A late answer to a round is accepted only while the session is running.

Proposed, pending the owner

5. Should late answers ever reach Workie's read?

Not by default. They are listed beside the read, not in it. A host who wants them in presses Redo; the redone read includes them and says so in a line.

Proposed, pending the owner

6. Offer "Ask the room" when Workie made no suggestions?

Yes: offer the blank composer whenever questions for the room are switched on for that environment. The switch itself stays as it is.

Proposed, pending the owner

7. Replace "Request feedback" with the "Comments" kind inside Ask the room?

Yes. One door for asking the room anything. The composer states the difference: comments carry names; Ideas, Choose and Rate are anonymous.

Proposed, pending the owner

8. Mid-read "Start round 3": a second press while questions are unsent, or move it into the session menu?

The second press. It keeps today's tap count for a host who wants to move on, and stops the accidental skip the reviewer hit in rounds 3 and 6.

Proposed, pending the owner

9. Which settings may change while the room is live?

Session name and room screen: at once. Categories, question picks and round count: from the next round. The live question, its answers and its ballots: never from Settings. A future "reopen" would be its own act that says what it does to answers already in.

Raised by the owner's updated spec.

Proposed, pending the owner

10. On one screen, may the host look at other steps on the projected laptop?

No. On one screen the strip only explains a step, because a private view would be on the wall. Looking happens on the Remote. If the host has no Remote, Setup and Settings open as today, and the room sees them.

Proposed, pending the owner

11. Can the host ever see who sent an anonymous comment?

No. The name is not stored with the comment, so the host, the wall and the report cannot show it. Membership is still checked when it is sent, so only people in the room can post.

Proposed, pending the owner

12. If the host turns on anonymous feedback partway through, what happens to comments already sent with names?

They become anonymous on the wall and in the report from then on; the names stay with the comments, so turning it off again restores them. Comments the writer sent anonymously stay anonymous whatever the host does.

Proposed, pending the owner

13. In a round that hid names, do earlier-round views and comment lines hide them too?

Yes. Responses read "Response 3", comment lines "[Jake commented on Response 3]". The commenter's own name still follows the feedback setting.

Proposed, pending the owner

14. Do participants see the review labels?

No. Labels are the host's bookkeeping and the report's. Participants see the comments, never whether the host has reviewed them.

Call and Answer: one round, moment by moment

Version 2 of the proposed flow for a Call and Answer round in Engage. It answers the external review of session 7924 (the "Relay expansion" run, six rounds, four participants) and redraws the reviewers' concept with the team's decisions applied. Every screen below is a wireframe: it shows what each surface shows, where things sit, and the one next step at each moment. Colours and fonts are not the point.

Prepared 14 October 2026 for the product owner, then the external reviewers. Sample content is from the review session; names are the reviewers' fictional participants.

What changed since the reviewers' version

  • One "Ask the room" composer, two ways in. Blank from the dock, or filled in by Workie from a small icon beside a point of the read. Same screen either way; the words are editable at once, then "Send to the room".
  • Your format cards are kept as the composer's kind switch (Ideas, Choose, Rate, Comments). "Comments" replaces "Request feedback", so there is one door for asking the room anything.
  • The end of the read is a real stop. On the last page the main button becomes "Ask the room". Mid-read, "Next round" needs a second press while questions are still unsent. Paging stays in the fixed button bar, because clickers and the Space key depend on it.
  • Labels stay short ("Start voting", "Show results"). The status line beside them says what comes next, instead of long button labels. No six-step route strip; the status line and "Round 2 of 6" carry "where am I".
  • Nothing that arrives late is lost. Late answers, ideas and comments are kept and marked "Not reviewed by the team" until the host reads them or shows them.
  • Anyone can go back to an earlier round and comment, on their phone, laptop or tablet, or from the host's History. The wall shows nothing unless the host puts it up.
  • The report is a full record of each question for the room: Workie's words against what was sent, where in the read it came from, timings, results and how many answered.
  • Kept from your version: the real task on the wall, the room-screen choice before round 1, "Preparing questions" then "3 asks ready", "Checking your answer…" after a reload, and a privacy line for each kind.

How to read this page

Each moment shows the surfaces side by side. A coloured square in each caption says which surface it is. The orange bar above each row is the one next step for the host at that moment.

Host laptop

The host's main controls. A fixed button bar at the bottom: a status line, a second (quiet) button and one main button.

Host phone (Remote)

An optional remote control on the host's own phone. Same buttons, same rules as the laptop. It also carries the host-only notes.

The wall (Presentation window)

What the room sees on the projector or TV. Never shows host controls, names in anonymous moments, or anything not yet sent.

Participant

Each person in the room, on their own phone, laptop or tablet. Drawn phone-sized; the same page fits a laptop or tablet.

Report

The session report the host opens, saves and shares after the session.

Two screens (the main reference here)

The laptop is the host's private dashboard. The room watches a separate Presentation window on a second screen, opened with "Open Present". Host-only things (Workie's reasons, the icons beside points, late items) stay on the laptop.

One screen (marked One screen)

The laptop itself is projected, so the room sees whatever the laptop shows. The flow is the same, with less on the laptop: no icons beside points, Workie's reasons folded away, late items shown as a count until opened. The Remote, if open, carries the host-only detail.

Direction 4 · Late answers, ideas and comments are kept and labelled

"Late" means anything that reaches the server after its moment has closed. Today these are dropped or refused, and in one case the phone still shows a receipt. In this version they are kept with their round, never counted, voted on or added to Workie's read, and carry one of three labels everywhere.

The three labels (for late items only)

Not reviewed by the teamThe default. Nobody has looked at it during the session.
Read by the hostThe host opened it and pressed "Mark as read", but did not show it.
Shown to the roomThe host put it on the wall. The only label that means the room saw it.

On-time items carry no label: they are already in the round. An on-time idea the host never picked is simply "Not shown", a different state.

List every idea, anonymous, in two groups: "Shown to the room" and "Not shown". Freeze them on the question when it closes so they outlast the short-lived answer rows. Keep the wall picked-only.

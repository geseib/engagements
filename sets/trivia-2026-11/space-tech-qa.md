- No false correct answer, duplicate valid answer among offered options, or date-sensitive “current record” trap found in the full read.
- Science wording appropriately distinguishes Venus sidereal spin from solar day, Moon phases from eclipses, orbital free fall from zero gravity, and comet ion tails from travel trails. Technology avoids equating HTTPS with truthful content or probable language with verified facts.

- Easy items intentionally remain; the content is suitable for mixed-age groups rather than a specialist exam. No audience playtest or UI rendering test was performed by this reviewer.
